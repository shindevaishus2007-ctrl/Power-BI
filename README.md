## 🎀 Dior Makeup Performance Dashboard 🎀.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze commercial retail data and provide meaningful insights into sales performance, product trends, customer demographics, buying behavior, marketing effectiveness, and inventory health.
By synthesizing these multi-dimensional data points into a unified reporting tool, the project aims to drive omni-channel growth, enhance customer loyalty, and streamline boutique supply chain operations.

The Power BI report will help marketing, sales, and retail operations teams understand:

• Total revenue, units sold, and Average Order Value (AOV).

• Sales distribution across cosmetic categories (Lips, Face, Eyes, Fragrance).

• Performance variations between Online and Boutique purchasing channels.

• Regional sales trends and product profit margins.

• Customer segments based on age groups and loyalty tiers (Platinum, Gold, Silver).

• Customer satisfaction (CSAT) and product return rates.

• Marketing campaign ROI and social media platform conversion rates.

• Inventory turnover ratios and out-of-stock rates across boutique locations.
